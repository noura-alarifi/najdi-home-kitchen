About Our Chef

Najdi Home Kitchen was founded by Chef Amad Al-Mutairi, a passionate cook from the heart of Riyadh who has dedicated her life to preserving the authentic flavors of Najdi cuisine. Her recipes are inspired by the heritage of her ancestors, blending traditional techniques with modern presentation.

What started as a small family kitchen has now become one of Riyadh’s favorite destinations for homemade dishes like jareesh, qursan, and margoug. Chef Amad believes that food is not just about taste — it is about preserving culture and sharing love with every meal.

Our Culinary Journey

Year Milestone
2015 Chef Amad began serving homemade Najdi dishes to local families in Riyadh.
2018 Najdi Home Kitchen was officially registered as a family business.
2020 Featured in “Taste of Riyadh” culinary festival for promoting Saudi heritage.
2025 Expanded to offer online delivery across Riyadh, serving thousands of happy customers.

Our Core Values

  • Authenticity: Every dish reflects true Najdi heritage.
  • Family: Meals cooked with love, inspired by generations.
  • Quality: Fresh, locally sourced ingredients only.
  • Community: Promoting Riyadh’s culinary identity worldwide.
